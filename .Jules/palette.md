## 2026-07-01 - [Registration Form UX Enhancement]
**Learning:** Replacing browser native alerts with inline, animated success states (framer-motion) significantly improves the perceived quality and continuity of the user experience, especially when combined with proper ARIA-compliant labeling.
**Action:** Always prefer inline success/feedback UI over native alerts; ensure every form input has a corresponding 'id' and 'htmlFor' label for accessibility.
