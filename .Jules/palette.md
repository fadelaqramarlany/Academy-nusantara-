## 2026-03-29 - Improving Form Accessibility and Feedback
**Learning:** Linking labels to inputs via `htmlFor`/`id` and adding `cursor-pointer` significantly improves the perceived quality and accessibility of forms. Replacing native `alert()` calls with inline animated success states provides a smoother, more integrated user experience that doesn't disrupt the flow.
**Action:** Always ensure form labels are properly linked and provide delightful feedback for successful submissions using existing animation libraries.
