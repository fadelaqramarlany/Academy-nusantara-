## 2026-06-19 - [Inline Success States & A11y]
**Learning:** Replacing native browser alerts with inline success states using Framer Motion improves perceived quality and user flow. Additionally, explicit label-to-input association via id/htmlFor is essential for screen reader users and form focusability.
**Action:** Always prefer inline conditional rendering for form feedback and ensure all inputs have corresponding labels with matching IDs.
