## 2026-06-11 - [Registration Form UX & A11y]
**Learning:** Replacing native browser alerts with inline success states and celebratory feedback (confetti) significantly improves user delight. Linking labels to inputs via id/htmlFor and providing tactile feedback (active:scale-95) are essential micro-UX patterns for interactive forms.
**Action:** Apply consistent tactile feedback and inline success states to all future form implementations in this repository.
