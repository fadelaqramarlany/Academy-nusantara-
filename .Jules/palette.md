## 2026-04-09 - Registration Form Micro-UX
**Learning:** Replacing native alerts with inline animated success states significantly improves user flow and perceived quality. Proper label-input linking is a fundamental accessibility requirement often missed in rapid prototyping.
**Action:** Implement 'AnimatePresence' for form success states and ensure all form elements have explicit 'id' and 'htmlFor' associations with interactive cursors.
