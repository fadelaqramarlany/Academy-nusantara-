## 2025-05-14 - Form Accessibility and Build Integrity
**Learning:** Proper form accessibility (linking labels to inputs) is a fundamental but often overlooked micro-UX improvement. Additionally, ensuring the main entry point (index.tsx) is correctly linked in index.html is critical for application function and build verification.
**Action:** Always check for missing id/htmlFor in forms and verify the HTML entry point in new or broken Vite projects.
