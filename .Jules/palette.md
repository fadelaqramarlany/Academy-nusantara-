## 2025-05-14 - Accessible Navigation and Forms
**Learning:** For a truly accessible Indonesian interface, navigation and form controls must have descriptive labels in the local language ('Buka menu', 'Hubungi kami di WhatsApp') and explicit label-input associations using `htmlFor` and `id`.
**Action:** Always verify that icon-only buttons have `aria-label` and that all form fields are explicitly linked to their labels to support both screen readers and a larger click target for all users.
