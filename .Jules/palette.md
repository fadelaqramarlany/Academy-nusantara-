## 2026-06-05 - Inline Success States for Registration
**Learning:** Replacing native browser alerts with inline success states significantly improves the user's sense of continuity and trust. Using Framer Motion for a subtle entrance animation (scale-up) makes the confirmation feel more "official" and rewarding.
**Action:** Prioritize surgical conditional rendering for form success states. Always include a reset action (e.g., "Daftar Lagi") to ensure the UI remains usable without a page refresh.

## 2026-06-05 - Tactile Feedback for Navigation and Forms
**Learning:** Adding 'active:scale-95 transition-all' to primary action buttons and navigation links provides immediate physical-like feedback, which is especially important for landing pages with many interactive elements.
**Action:** Apply consistent tactile feedback patterns to all Link and Button components to enhance the "premium" feel of the interface.
