## 2026-04-09 - [Accessibility and Tactile Feedback in App Shell]
**Learning:** Adding a "Skip to main content" link and ARIA labels to icon-only buttons significantly improves the experience for keyboard and screen reader users. Combining this with tactile feedback (`active:scale-95`) provides a more responsive feel for touch and mouse users.
**Action:** Always include skip links in the main layout and ensure icon-only buttons have descriptive ARIA labels and active states.
