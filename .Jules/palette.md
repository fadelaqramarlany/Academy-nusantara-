## 2025-05-14 - Inline Success State and Tactile Feedback
**Learning:** Replacing generic browser alerts with inline success messages significantly improves the perceived quality and continuity of the user experience. Adding tactile feedback (active:scale-95) provides immediate physical-like confirmation of interaction, which is especially important for accessibility and mobile users.
**Action:** Always prefer conditional rendering for form states over `alert()` and ensure every interactive element has a clear tactile feedback class.
