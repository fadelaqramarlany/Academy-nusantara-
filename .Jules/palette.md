## 2026-04-09 - Improve Contact Form UX and Accessibility
**Learning:** Browser `alert()` calls disrupt the user experience and feel dated. Inline success states with visual feedback (icons, animations) provide a much more pleasant and modern interaction. Linking labels to inputs via `id`/`htmlFor` and adding tactile feedback are small but high-impact accessibility and UX improvements.
**Action:** Replace `alert()` with inline success messages using Framer Motion, ensure all form fields have proper accessibility associations, and add tactile feedback to interactive elements.
