## 2025-05-15 - Global Navigation Accessibility & Tactile Feedback
**Learning:** Adding a "Skip to main content" link and enhancing button interactivity with tactile feedback (scale-down on click) significantly improves the perceived quality and accessibility of the global navigation without cluttering the UI. Standardizing these patterns ensures a consistent and inclusive experience.
**Action: ** Implement "Skip to main content" links and `active:scale-95` tactile feedback for all primary navigation and call-to-action elements in upcoming tasks to maintain high UX standards.
