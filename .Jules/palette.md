## 2026-04-09 - Improving ChatBot Interactivity and Accessibility
**Learning:** Native `confirm()` dialogs break the immersive feel of a "PRO ENGINE" AI interface. Inline confirmations or tactile feedback are preferred.
**Action:** Replace `confirm()` in ChatBot with a two-state confirmation button and add missing ARIA labels/tactile feedback.
