## 2025-05-18 - [Accessibility and Interaction Polish]
**Learning:** Browser alerts are jarring; replacing them with inline animated success states (framer-motion) significantly improves perceived quality. A "Skip to main content" link is essential for keyboard navigation in SPA layouts.
**Action:** Always check for native alert() usage and missing skip-links in new projects. Use active:scale-95 for consistent tactile feedback on all buttons.
