## 2026-04-09 - [Registration Form Micro-UX & Accessibility]
**Learning:** Replacing native browser `alert()` with inline success states improves user flow and brand consistency. Accessibility is improved by properly linking labels to inputs and adding interactive cues like `cursor-pointer`.
**Action:** Use conditional rendering with Framer Motion for success states and ensure all form elements have unique `id`s and matching `htmlFor` labels.
