## 2026-04-09 - Registration Form UX & Accessibility
**Learning:** Replacing native browser `alert()` with inline, animated success states significantly improves the "flow" of a single-page application, making it feel more integrated and modern. Adding tactile feedback (`active:scale-95`) and proper label linking (`htmlFor`) are essential micro-UX details that enhance both accessibility and the "feel" of interactivity.
**Action:** Always prefer inline success states over alerts and ensure every form field is correctly labeled and responsive to touch/click.
