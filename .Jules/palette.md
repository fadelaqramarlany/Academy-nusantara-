## 2025-05-15 - Accessibility and Tactile Feedback Pattern
**Learning:** Keyboard users benefit from a 'Skip to main content' link, and mobile users expect tactile feedback and clear ARIA states for navigation.
**Action:** Always include a skip link, use `active:scale-95 transition-all` for tactile feedback, and ensure mobile menus have proper `aria-label` and `aria-expanded` attributes.
