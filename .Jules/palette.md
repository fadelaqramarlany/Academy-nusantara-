## 2025-05-15 - [Inline Form Success State]
**Learning:** Replacing native browser `alert()` with an inline success state significantly improves the "flow" of a form submission and provides a more professional feel. Using `framer-motion` and `canvas-confetti` adds a delightful touch that makes the interaction memorable.
**Action:** Always prefer custom inline success states over browser alerts. Ensure layout stability by using `min-h` on the form container and use `AnimatePresence` for smooth transitions.

## 2025-05-15 - [Form Accessibility and Tactile Feedback]
**Learning:** Simple additions like `htmlFor`/`id` association for labels and `active:scale-95` for buttons have a high impact on usability with very little code.
**Action:** Always verify label-input associations and add tactile feedback to primary action buttons.
