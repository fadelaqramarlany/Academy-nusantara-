# Palette Journal - Academy Nusantara

## 2025-05-17 - Replacing native alerts and enhancing form accessibility
**Learning:** Native `alert()` calls disrupt the user flow and feel disconnected from the application's modern UI. Inline success states with animations provide a more integrated and "delightful" experience. Additionally, explicit `id`/`htmlFor` linking and tactile feedback (`active:scale-95`) significantly improve accessibility and perceived responsiveness.
**Action:** Always prefer inline success states over `alert()` for form submissions. Ensure all form fields are properly labeled with `id` and `htmlFor`, and interactive elements provide immediate visual feedback on click.
