## 2025-05-15 - Contact Form Micro-UX Enhancements
**Learning:** A simple `alert()` for form submission is jarring. A state-driven success UI with animations and celebratory confetti provides a more integrated and pleasant feedback experience. Accessibility is also key; ensuring labels are correctly linked to inputs via `id` and `htmlFor` is a fundamental requirement.
**Action:** Replace `alert()` with an animated success state using `framer-motion` and `canvas-confetti`. Fix accessibility by linking labels to inputs.
