## 2026-04-09 - Form UX & Accessibility Improvement
**Learning:** Browser `alert()` calls are disruptive to the user flow. Replacing them with inline success states using Framer Motion provides a much more polished and modern feel. Additionally, proper label-input association (`htmlFor`/`id`) is critical for accessibility but often overlooked in rapid development.
**Action:** Replace `alert()` with conditional rendering of a success state in forms, and ensure all form fields have linked labels and tactile feedback.
