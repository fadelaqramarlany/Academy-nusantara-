## 2025-05-15 - [Enhanced Registration Success State]
**Learning:** Replacing native browser `alert()` with a contextual, animated success state (using Framer Motion's AnimatePresence) significantly improves the perceived quality and flow of the registration process.
**Action:** Always prefer inline success states with visual confirmation (like 'CircleCheck') and clear follow-up actions (like 'Daftar Siswa Lain') over intrusive modal alerts.
