# Palette's Journal - Academy Nusantara

## 2025-05-14 - Foundation of Accessibility and Tactile Feedback
**Learning:** In a high-energy, "maximalist" UI like Academy Nusantara (heavy on black, emerald, and bold typography), micro-interactions like tactile feedback (scale-down on click) are essential to make the app feel "alive" and responsive. Furthermore, standard accessibility features like "Skip to Content" are often overlooked in single-page landing sites but are critical for keyboard-only navigation.
**Action:** Always implement 'active:scale-95 transition-all' for interactive elements and ensure a 'Lanjut ke konten utama' link is present in the main Layout/App component.
