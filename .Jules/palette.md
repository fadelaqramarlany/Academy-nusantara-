## 2025-05-14 - [Enhancing Form Accessibility and Tactile Feedback]
**Learning:** Explicitly linking labels to inputs via `htmlFor` and `id` is crucial for both screen reader accessibility and improving the clickable area for users. Additionally, providing tactile feedback via `active:scale-95` significantly enhances the "feel" of interaction in a web application.
**Action:** Always verify that every form field has a linked label and that interactive elements have appropriate active states for feedback.
